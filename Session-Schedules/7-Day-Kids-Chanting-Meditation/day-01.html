7-Day Kids Chanting & Silent Meditation • Day 1

Sahana Vavatu – Learning Together

Topic

This is a beautiful prayer we say when we learn together — student and teacher, or friends helping each other. It asks that we stay protected, grow strong in knowledge, and never quarrel.

Today we remember that learning is happiest when we do it with kindness and togetherness.

Mantra

Sanskrit

ओम सह नाववतु ।
सह नौ भुनक्तु ।
सह वीर्यं करवावहै ।
तेजस्वि नावधीतमस्तु मा विद्विषावहै ।
ओम शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥

Transliteration

Om Saha Nāvavatu
Saha Nau Bhunaktu
Saha Vīryam Karavāvahai
Tejasvi Nāvadhītamastu Mā Vidviṣāvahai
Om Śāntiḥ Śāntiḥ Śāntiḥ

Simple meaning

May we be protected together.
May we grow and enjoy learning together.
May we work with energy and strength.
May our learning shine brightly and may we never quarrel.
Om. Peace, peace, peace.

Practice

Sit comfortably with a straight, relaxed back. Soften the shoulders and face.

  • Chant the mantra slowly together 3 to 11 times. Feel the sound in the body.
  • When finished, sit in silence for about 15 minutes.
  • Simply notice the breath coming in and going out. If thoughts appear, gently come back to the breath — no force, just kindness.

After the silence, open the eyes slowly and smile.
